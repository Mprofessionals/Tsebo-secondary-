About Tsebo Secondary School

Empowering the Youth of Phuthaditjhaba with Knowledge & Vision.

Located on Motebang Road in Ha-Rankopane Village, Phuthaditjhaba, Tsebo Secondary School is a cornerstone of secondary education in the Thabo Mofutsanyana district. Our motto, "Tsebo ke Lesedi" (Knowledge is Light), drives our collective commitment to academic rigor, strong moral values, and student empowerment.

Official School Uniform

The Tsebo uniform signifies dignity, equality, and pride. All learners must adhere strictly to the dress code.

Boys School Uniform

  • Royal Blue Blazer with official embroidered Tsebo crest
  • Plain White Shirt (Collared, tucked in at all times)
  • Standard Grey School Trousers with plain black belt
  • Official School Tie (Royal Blue & Gold stripe)
  • Black Polishable Shoes with grey socks

Girls School Uniform

  • Royal Blue Blazer with official embroidered Tsebo crest
  • Plain White Blouse (Collared)
  • Grey Pleated Skirt (Below knee length) or Grey Slacks
  • Official School Tie (Royal Blue & Gold stripe)
  • Black Polishable Shoes with white socks (navy tights in winter)

Winter & Sports Attire

  • V-Neck Royal Blue Jersey or sleeveless pullover with gold trim
  • Official School Tracksuit for Phuthaditjhaba winter mornings
  • Sports Team Kit (Royal blue and gold athletic shirts)
